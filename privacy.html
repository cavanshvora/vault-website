VAULT FD MANAGEMENT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

VAULT FD Management ("VAULT", "we", "our", or "the application") is a Fixed Deposit management application designed to help users organize and track their financial records.

2. Information Stored in VAULT

Depending on the features used, users may enter information such as:

  • Account holder names
  • Bank and branch information
  • Fixed Deposit numbers
  • Deposit amounts
  • Interest rates
  • Deposit dates
  • Maturity dates
  • Maturity amounts
  • Personal notes entered by the user

3. Local Data

VAULT is designed to allow users to manage their Fixed Deposit records on their device. Users are responsible for the accuracy and security of information entered into the application.

4. Google Services

VAULT may provide optional integration with Google services, including Google Drive and Google Calendar.

When a user chooses to connect a Google account, VAULT may request permissions required to provide the selected functionality. These permissions are requested through Google's authentication system.

VAULT does not request access to Google services unless that access is required for a feature selected by the user.

5. Google User Data

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the functionality described by the application, such as backup, synchronization, or calendar-related features.

VAULT does not sell Google user data or use Google user data for advertising purposes.

6. Data Sharing

VAULT does not sell users' personal or financial information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, through an integrated service, or where required by applicable law.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device, application access, and connected accounts.

8. Children's Privacy

VAULT is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or VAULT, please contact us using the information provided on our Contact page.
