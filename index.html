Vault FD Management

A simple way to organize and manage your fixed deposit records.

Vault FD Management is a personal financial record-management application that helps users keep track of their fixed deposits, maturity dates, interest information, and important reminders in one organized place.

About Vault FD Management

Vault FD Management helps users organize information about their fixed deposits and other financial records. Users can maintain details such as deposit amount, bank, account or deposit number, interest rate, tenure, maturity date, maturity amount, and related notes.

The application is designed to make it easier to monitor financial records and remember important maturity dates and reminders.

What Vault FD Management Does

Fixed Deposit Management

Store and organize fixed deposit information in one place.

Maturity Tracking

Keep track of maturity dates and important deposit milestones.

Interest Information

Record interest rates, maturity amounts, and related financial information.

Reminders

Use reminders to help remember upcoming fixed deposit maturities and other important dates.

Google Services

When enabled by the user, Vault can use Google services such as Google Drive and Google Calendar to provide backup and calendar reminder functionality.

Personal Organization

Keep financial records organized so they are easier to review and manage.

Why Vault Uses Google Services

Vault FD Management may request access to Google services when a user chooses to connect their Google account.

Google Drive access may be used to support backup or synchronization of the user's Vault data. Google Calendar access may be used to create and manage reminders for important dates, such as fixed deposit maturities.

Google account access is optional and is used only when the user chooses to connect their Google account and grants the requested permissions.
